Overview

About Cloudinary Auto-Uploader

High-volume multi-archive video ingestion engine with automated text-to-keyword extraction and Cloudinary cloud syncing.

Managing and tagging hundreds of video assets manually is tedious, slow, and error-prone. Cloudinary Auto-Uploader solves this by taking raw .zip archives containing videos alongside their descriptive .txt or .json notes, automatically parsing titles, descriptions, and hashtags, and streaming them directly into Cloudinary with rich metadata and searchable tags.

Multi-ZIP Batch Processing

Upload multiple archives simultaneously up to 10GB. The system unpacks nested directory structures recursively without requiring any pre-formatting.

Smart Metadata Pairing

An intelligent heuristic algorithm matches videos with their neighboring text notes, extracting explicit keyphrases, social hashtags (#tags), or natural language keywords.

Resilient Chunked Streaming

Utilizes 10MB chunked streaming via Cloudinary's native upload engine, ensuring even massive 4K/60fps video files upload reliably without network timeouts.

Automatic Cloud Tagging

Extracted keywords are registered directly as Cloudinary asset tags, while titles and descriptions are indexed as searchable context metadata.
